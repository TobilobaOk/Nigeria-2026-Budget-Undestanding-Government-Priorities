# Nigeria-2026-Budget-Undestanding-Government-Priorities

**Project Overview**

This project presents an analytical exploration of Nigeria’s 2026 national budget using Power BI. The dashboard was developed to simplify complex government budget allocations into interactive visual insights that highlight spending priorities, sector allocation patterns, and potential impacts on citizens and national development.

The analysis focuses on understanding how public funds are distributed across critical sectors such as security, education, healthcare, agriculture, infrastructure, and public administration. Rather than focusing solely on raw numbers, the project emphasizes data storytelling, transparency, and insight generation.

**Problem Statement**

Government budget documents are often lengthy, technical, and difficult for the average citizen to interpret. This project aims to transform Nigeria’s 2026 budget data into a more understandable and interactive format that answers key questions such as:

•Which sectors received the highest government allocations?<br>
•What do these allocations reveal about national priorities?<br>
•Does spending align with Nigeria’s economic and social challenges?<br>
•What impact could these allocations have on citizens and national development?<br>

**Objectives**

The objectives of this project are to:

•Analyze budget allocation patterns across major ministries and sectors<br>
•Identify sectors receiving the highest government investment<br>
•Examine the relationship between spending priorities and national challenges<br>
•Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for budget exploration<br>
•Generate insights that support transparency and public understanding<br>

**Dataset Information**

The dataset was sourced from the official Nigerian Budget Office.

**Data Source**

Budget Office of the Federation Nigeria

The original data was extracted from the 2026 Appropriation Budget document provided in PDF format.

**Tools & Technologies Used**

•Microsoft Power BI<br>
•Power Query<br>
•Microsoft Excel<br>
•DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)<br>

**Data Cleaning & Preparation**

The original dataset was extracted from PDF budget documents and transformed into structured CSV files for analysis.

Data preparation steps included:

•Extracting tables from PDF documents<br>
•Standardizing column headers<br>
•Removing null and empty values<br>
•Merging duplicate ministry codes<br>
•Structuring sector allocations for analysis<br>
•Creating DAX measures for KPIs and visualizations<br>

Although the dataset contained formatting inconsistencies, sufficient cleaning and transformation were carried out to support meaningful analysis and dashboard development.

**Key Insights**

**Security Dominates Government Spending**

The security sector received the largest share of the national budget, indicating the government's strong focus on addressing national security concerns and maintaining stability.

**Infrastructure Remains a National Priority**

Significant allocations toward works and infrastructure suggest continued investment in economic development and public infrastructure projects.

**Social Sectors Continue to Receive Attention**

Education, healthcare, and agriculture received notable funding allocations, reflecting their importance to human capital development and food security.

**High Personnel & Overhead Costs**

A considerable portion of government expenditure is directed toward personnel and overhead costs, highlighting the operational demands of public administration.

**Project Limitations**

The analysis was conducted using publicly available budget documents extracted from PDF files. Due to formatting inconsistencies within the original source documents, some records required manual transformation and consolidation during preprocessing.

As a result, the project focuses primarily on high-level analytical insights and spending patterns rather than financial auditing or exact budget verification.

**Conclusion**

This project demonstrates how data analytics and visualization can simplify complex government financial data into accessible insights for citizens, analysts, and decision-makers.

The dashboard reveals that Nigeria’s 2026 budget strongly prioritizes security, infrastructure, and administrative spending while also allocating substantial resources to education, healthcare, and agriculture. These spending patterns provide insight into the country’s economic direction and national development priorities.

By leveraging Power BI for storytelling and visualization, this project promotes transparency, public understanding, and data-driven discussions around government spending.
