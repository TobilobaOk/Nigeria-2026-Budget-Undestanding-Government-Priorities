# Nigeria-2026-Budget-Undestanding-Government-Priorities

**Project Overview**

This project presents an analytical exploration of Nigeria’s 2026 national budget using Power BI. The dashboard was developed to simplify complex government budget allocations into interactive visual insights that highlight spending priorities, sector allocation patterns, and potential impacts on citizens and national development.

The analysis focuses on understanding how public funds are distributed across critical sectors such as security, education, healthcare, agriculture, infrastructure, and public administration. Rather than focusing solely on raw numbers, the project emphasizes data storytelling, transparency, and insight generation.

**Problem Statement**

Government budget documents are often lengthy, technical, and difficult for the average citizen to interpret. This project aims to transform Nigeria’s 2026 budget data into a more understandable and interactive format that answers key questions such as:

•Which sectors received the highest government allocations?<br>
•What do these allocations reveal about national priorities?<br>
•Does spending align with Nigeria’s economic and social challenges?<br>
•What impact could these allocations have on citizens and national development?<br>
